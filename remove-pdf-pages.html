Common questions

Remove PDF Pages FAQs

Is this really free?
Yes — there's no signup, no watermark, no page limit and no hidden paywall. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there's no server cost on our end to pass on to you.
Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?
No. Editing happens entirely on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your file is never sent to Nexsia or any third party — closing the tab is the same as deleting it.
Can I undo a page removal?
Yes — click the × again on a page you marked for removal to restore it, any time before you click "Save PDF". Nothing is permanent until you download the result.
Does this work with password-protected PDFs?
No — a password-protected PDF can't be read by the browser to generate thumbnails or apply changes. Remove the password using your PDF viewer first, then use this tool.
Will the quality or formatting change?
No. The remaining pages are copied directly from the original PDF's internal structure, not re-rendered as images, so text stays selectable and quality is identical to the source.
