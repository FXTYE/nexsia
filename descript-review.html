Common questions

Descript FAQs

Is Descript free?
Yes, there's a free plan with limited monthly hours and a watermark on some exports — enough to try the workflow. Paid plans start around $16/month.
Is Descript good for YouTube?
Especially for talking-head, tutorial and podcast channels. Text-based editing and AI cleanup make it fast for dialogue-heavy video. Cinematic, effects-heavy work still suits a timeline editor.
Does Descript replace Premiere Pro?
For most creators' needs, largely yes — but for multi-layer effects and colour grading, Premiere gives more control. Many creators use Descript for speed and a timeline editor for the rest.
What is Overdub?
An AI feature that lets you fix audio mistakes by typing the correction, which Descript voices in a matching voice. Handy for small fixes without re-recording.
Is the affiliate payout recurring?
No — Descript's affiliate program pays a one-time commission rather than recurring. It still converts well thanks to broad appeal.
