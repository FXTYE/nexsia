The quick verdict

SiteGround genuinely earns its reputation as the premium, support-first host — Google Cloud infrastructure, a polished custom control panel, and the most consistently praised support team in this entire category back that up across independent sources.

The honest caveat: SiteGround's renewal pricing is genuinely steep — StartUp jumps from ~$3/month to $17.99-24.99/month depending on your term length, a 400-500%+ increase. And the entry StartUp plan caps you at a single website, which surprises people who assume "shared hosting" means unlimited sites. If support quality and Google Cloud reliability matter more to you than the lowest possible price, SiteGround is worth the premium — just go in knowing the real renewal number, not the promotional one.

Know before you buy

The renewal jump, honestly

This is the detail SiteGround's own marketing tends to bury in a footnote. The StartUp plan's promotional $2.99-3.99/month price is real for your first term, but the renewal rate — the number you'll actually pay every year after — sits meaningfully higher, and the exact figure depends on which billing term you originally chose.

Renewal ranges from $14.99 to $24.99/month depending on term

A 24-month term renews around $14.99/month, a 12-month term around $17.99/month, and month-to-month billing renews at $24.99/month — the same StartUp plan, three different renewal prices. The longer term you commit to upfront, the lower your eventual renewal rate, which is the opposite of how most people budget for renewals.

Two more things worth knowing: the StartUp plan is capped at a single website — if you're planning more than one site, you need GrowBig, which costs noticeably more at every stage. And GrowBig is genuinely where most of SiteGround's best features live (staging, priority support, more resources) — StartUp is a real entry tier, not the full experience.

Common questions

SiteGround FAQs

Is SiteGround worth the higher price?
Yes, if support quality and reliability matter more to you than the lowest possible cost — SiteGround's support and Google Cloud infrastructure genuinely outperform cheaper competitors. If price is your main constraint, Hostinger or Bluehost cost meaningfully less at every tier.
Why does SiteGround's renewal price vary so much?
SiteGround's renewal rate depends on which billing term you originally chose — a 24-month term renews lower (~$14.99/mo for StartUp) than a 12-month term (~$17.99/mo) or month-to-month (~$24.99/mo). The same plan has three different renewal prices depending on your original commitment.
Can I host more than one website on StartUp?
No — StartUp is genuinely limited to a single website. If you're planning multiple sites, even just two, you'll need to upgrade to GrowBig, which supports unlimited websites.
SiteGround vs Hostinger — which is better?
SiteGround wins on support quality, infrastructure, and managed WordPress features; Hostinger wins decisively on price at every tier. If you're price-sensitive and comfortable managing things yourself, Hostinger is the better value. If support and reliability matter more, SiteGround is worth the premium. See our full comparison.
Does SiteGround offer phone support?
Yes — SiteGround offers live chat, tickets, and phone support, making it the most complete support coverage among the three major budget-to-mid-tier hosts compared here.
Is SiteGround good for WordPress?
Yes, genuinely — SiteGround is one of the most WordPress-focused hosts in this category, with built-in staging, WP-CLI access on GoGeek, and consistently strong performance benchmarks for WordPress specifically.
