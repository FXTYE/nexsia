The quick verdict

Crisp earns its spot as our per-workspace value pick because it solves the seat-counting problem outright — you get a bundle of agent seats included in every tier rather than paying per person. Hugo AI's credit-based system, included from the Mini plan up, is also a more transparent way to price AI support than treating it as a bolt-on subscription. The trade-off is that real functionality (knowledge base, CRM, WhatsApp) doesn't unlock until Essentials at $95/mo, and Hugo simply stops responding once your credits are spent for the period.

Common questions

Crisp FAQs

Is Crisp free?
Yes — the free plan includes 2 live chat seats with no time limit, though AI and workflow features require a paid tier.
How does Crisp's per-workspace pricing work?
Instead of billing per agent, Crisp bundles a set number of seats into each tier (2 on Free, 4 on Mini, 10 on Essentials, 20+ on Plus), with extra agents at $10/mo each beyond that.
What happens when Hugo AI runs out of credits?
Hugo stops responding to new conversations until your credits reset the following billing period, unless you purchase additional credits.
Crisp vs Intercom — which is better?
Crisp wins on price and per-workspace flexibility for smaller teams; Intercom wins on brand maturity, live chat sophistication and Fin AI's resolution quality. Crisp is the stronger pick if budget and seat-count flexibility matter most.
Does Crisp work well for ecommerce?
It's usable for small ecommerce sites, but Gorgias is the more purpose-built option if you're specifically on Shopify and want deep order-data integration.
