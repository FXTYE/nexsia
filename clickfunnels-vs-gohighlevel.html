In depth

Pros & cons of each platform

CF ClickFunnels

Pro

More polished funnel builder

Conversion-tested templates, faster page rendering and a cleaner, more design-focused editor. GoHighLevel's builder is capable but more complex and less refined.

Pro

Simpler to learn

It does one main job, so there's far less to configure. You can launch a funnel quickly without wading through a sprawling interface.

Pro

Strong community & ecosystem

A large, active community built around the "funnel hacker" philosophy, with plenty of templates, share funnels and training.

Con

No real CRM, SMS or booking

Beyond basic contacts it has no full CRM, native SMS, or scheduling — you'll pay for external tools to cover what GoHighLevel includes.

Con

No white-label & can't host client funnels

There's no standard white-label option, and ClickFunnels' terms explicitly prohibit hosting client funnels on your account — a dealbreaker for agencies.

HL GoHighLevel

Pro

Replaces a whole stack

Full CRM, email, native SMS, calendars, reputation management and funnels in one platform — consolidating tools you'd otherwise pay for separately.

Pro

White-label SaaS resale

Higher tiers let agencies rebrand the entire platform — domain, logo, pricing — and resell it to clients as their own software. ClickFunnels has no equivalent at any price.

Pro

Unlimited sub-accounts & contacts

Manage many clients with flat pricing as you grow, plus multi-channel automation across email, SMS, voice, WhatsApp and social.

Con

Steep learning curve

The first login shows 20-plus menu categories, and users routinely report one to three weeks to feel comfortable. It's a lot of platform.

Con

Less polished funnels & email setup

The funnel builder is more utilitarian, and email deliverability needs technical setup (and draws more complaints) than ClickFunnels' simpler approach.

The honest one-liner from the research: ClickFunnels is better at the one thing it was built for — building a sales funnel quickly. GoHighLevel does far more, for similar entry pricing, if you're willing to climb the learning curve.

Feature by feature

What each platform gives you

Funnel & page builder

ClickFunnels: polished, conversion-tested, faster rendering. GoHighLevel: capable but more complex, less design-focused.

CRM

GoHighLevel: full CRM — pipelines, tasks, notes, custom fields, built for multiple clients. ClickFunnels: basic contact management only.

Multi-channel automation

GoHighLevel: email, SMS, voice, WhatsApp and social. ClickFunnels: primarily email-based automation.

Calendars & booking

GoHighLevel: native scheduling built in. ClickFunnels: none — needs an external tool like Calendly.

White-label / resale

GoHighLevel: full SaaS Mode to rebrand and resell. ClickFunnels: no standard white-label; can't host client funnels.

Sub-accounts

GoHighLevel: unlimited client sub-accounts on higher tiers. ClickFunnels: workspaces cap at 1–20 by plan.

Ease of use

ClickFunnels: simple, fast to launch. GoHighLevel: powerful but a 1–3 week learning curve.

Free trial

Both offer a 14-day free trial (GoHighLevel often 30 days via partners). Test the workflow before committing.

Our verdict

ClickFunnels for polish, GoHighLevel for breadth. If your business is 'drive traffic to an offer through a funnel' and someone else handles follow-up, ClickFunnels' cleaner builder wins. If you want to replace a whole stack — CRM, SMS, calendars, funnels — or resell software to clients, GoHighLevel does far more for similar entry pricing.

The tell: ClickFunnels users often say they also pay for a CRM, a calendar tool and an email platform on the side; GoHighLevel users tend to run everything in one place. Agencies especially lean GoHighLevel for the white-label resale model ClickFunnels simply doesn't offer.

Common questions

ClickFunnels vs GoHighLevel FAQs

Is GoHighLevel better than ClickFunnels?
For breadth and agencies, yes — it bundles CRM, SMS, calendars and white-label resale. For a pure, polished funnel builder, many still prefer ClickFunnels. It depends on whether you want one tool or the best funnel tool.
Do they cost the same?
Both start around $97/mo, but that's misleading — GoHighLevel includes far more at that price, while ClickFunnels focuses on funnels. GoHighLevel's white-label SaaS Mode is a separate, higher tier (~$497/mo).
Can GoHighLevel replace ClickFunnels?
For most funnel needs, yes — it has a funnel builder plus much more. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve and some reports of weaker email deliverability.
Which is better for agencies?
GoHighLevel, clearly — its white-label SaaS Mode lets you resell the platform to clients under your own brand, which ClickFunnels has no equivalent for.
What about Kajabi?
Kajabi is a different category — courses and community, not funnels or agency CRM. See our ClickFunnels vs Kajabi comparison if course delivery matters to you.
