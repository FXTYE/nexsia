The quick verdict

CyberGhost is the budget-friendly, server-count-first pick in this category. Its purpose-picked server profiles are a genuinely nice touch for less experienced users who just want "a good server for Netflix" without researching locations, and the 45-day refund window is the longest of any VPN here. It doesn't match NordVPN, ExpressVPN or Surfshark on raw speed or advanced privacy tooling, and its 7-device cap trails Surfshark's unlimited policy — but for a low-commitment, easy-to-use VPN at a low price, it's a solid choice.

Common questions

CyberGhost FAQs

Is CyberGhost free?
No — there's no free tier, only a 45-day money-back guarantee on paid plans, which is longer than most rivals' 30-day window. ProtonVPN is the better option if you want a genuinely free VPN.
Does CyberGhost really have 11,000+ servers?
Yes — CyberGhost's server count across roughly 100 countries is among the largest of any consumer VPN, ahead of every other provider in this comparison.
CyberGhost vs Surfshark — which is better?
CyberGhost wins on raw server count and refund window length; Surfshark wins on unlimited devices and typically more consistent speeds. If device count matters most, go Surfshark. If server choice and a longer trial window matter more, go CyberGhost.
Does CyberGhost work with Netflix?
Yes, using its purpose-picked "streaming" server profiles — though NordVPN and Surfshark are generally more consistently reliable across regions.
How many devices can I use with one CyberGhost account?
Up to 7 simultaneous connections. If you need more, Surfshark's unlimited-device policy is the better fit.
