Common questions

ExpressVPN vs ProtonVPN FAQs

ExpressVPN vs ProtonVPN — which is better?
ExpressVPN wins on app polish and server network size; ProtonVPN wins on having a genuinely free tier and independently audited privacy.
Is ProtonVPN really free?
Yes — the free plan has no data cap or time limit, though it's limited to one device and fewer server locations.
Which has better apps?
ExpressVPN is generally considered more polished, though ProtonVPN's open-source apps are a plus for privacy-conscious users.
Which is more private?
ProtonVPN's no-logs policy has been independently audited multiple times, a higher bar than ExpressVPN's self-reported claims.
Which is cheaper?
Both start paid plans around the same price ($2.99/mo), but ProtonVPN also offers a genuinely free tier ExpressVPN doesn't have.
