The quick verdict

OutlierKit is a sharp, focused research tool for YouTubers who want ideas grounded in what's already overperforming. Common questions

OutlierKit FAQs

What does OutlierKit do?
It finds 'outlier' YouTube videos — ones getting far more views than a channel's average — and surfaces the title and thumbnail patterns behind them, so you can model what already works.
Is OutlierKit better than vidIQ?
Different jobs. OutlierKit is focused on outlier research and ideation; vidIQ is a deeper all-round suite. Many creators use OutlierKit alongside a keyword tool rather than instead of one.
Does OutlierKit have a free plan?
It offers a free trial to test the core research. Paid plans start around $29/month — confirm current pricing on their site.
Is it worth it for a new channel?
It can be — modelling proven outliers is a solid way to choose topics early. Just pair it with a free keyword tool for search data.
How's the affiliate program?
Open with instant approval and recurring commission (around 20% for 12 months, no cap) — one of the easier programs to join when you're starting out.
