Common questions

Higgsfield AI FAQs

Is Higgsfield free?
There's a free tier, and the entry Basic plan is $9/mo (annual) with 120 credits — enough for roughly 60 images or about seven fast videos a month. Plus ($39/mo annual) is where most regular creators land.
What is Soul ID?
Soul ID is Higgsfield's character-consistency system. You upload 3-5 reference photos of a face, it trains a reusable character model in about 5-10 minutes, and that character then stays consistent across Higgsfield's generation tools — used heavily for AI influencer and brand-character content.
Does Higgsfield use its own AI model?
No — Higgsfield is an aggregator. It bundles access to third-party models including Sora, Veo, Kling and Seedance, and adds its own tools on top (Soul ID, Lipsync Studio, upscaling) rather than training its own foundation video model.
Higgsfield vs Kling — which should I use?
If you need a consistent character across lots of content, or want access to several models in one place, Higgsfield's tools are worth the premium. If you mainly use one model and want the lowest cost per generation, subscribing to Kling directly is usually cheaper.
