Common questions

Favicon Generator FAQs

Is this really free?
Yes — there's no signup, no watermark and no hidden paywall. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there's no server cost on our end to pass on to you.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No. Every size is generated entirely on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your image is never sent to Nexsia or any third party — closing the tab is the same as deleting it.
What image should I start with?
A square image, at least 512×512px, with a transparent background if your logo isn't meant to sit on a solid colour block. SVG source works well too — it's rasterized to each target size. Non-square images are center-cropped to a square before resizing.
What's actually in the .ico file?
A real multi-resolution ICO — it embeds the 16×16, 32×32 and 48×48 versions in one file, which is what browsers expect from favicon.ico. It's built directly in JavaScript, not just a renamed PNG.
Do I need all of these files?
favicon.ico and the 32×32/16×16 PNGs cover browsers; apple-touch-icon.png covers iOS home-screen icons; the android-chrome sizes cover Android home-screen and PWA icons. Using all of them is the safest bet across devices, but if you only care about desktop browsers, favicon.ico alone is enough.
