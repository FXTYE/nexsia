The quick verdict

Intercom remains the platform most people picture when they think of modern live chat and in-app messaging, and Fin's resolution quality backs up that reputation. The real work is understanding how its two pricing layers stack: seat fees for your team, then Fin billed per outcome on top, with quirks like a 50-resolution monthly floor and no volume discount that can make costs less predictable than a flat-fee competitor. For a product-led SaaS business that wants best-in-class in-app messaging, it's still a strong default — just model the full bill before committing, not just the advertised seat price.

Common questions

Intercom FAQs

Is Intercom free?
No — there's no permanent free plan, only a free trial. Essential is currently priced at $19–29/agent/mo as part of a live pricing test.
How much does Fin AI actually cost?
$0.99 per resolution, or $9.99 for a qualified lead, billed separately from your seat plan. There's a 50-outcome monthly floor, no rollover for unused resolutions, and no volume discount.
Intercom vs Zendesk — which is better?
Intercom wins on live chat sophistication, in-app messaging and product-led growth tooling; Zendesk wins on enterprise customization, reporting depth and its larger app marketplace. Intercom tends to suit SaaS/product companies best.
Does Intercom have an affiliate program?
There's no confirmed public self-serve affiliate program as of 2026 — Intercom instead runs a Solution Partner program aimed at agencies and consultants rather than content creators or referral marketers.
Is Intercom good for ecommerce?
It can work, but Gorgias is the more purpose-built option for Shopify stores specifically, with order and shipping data surfaced natively inside every ticket.
