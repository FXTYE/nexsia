Common questions

JPG to PDF FAQs

Is this really free?
Yes — there's no signup, no watermark, no page limit and no hidden paywall. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there's no server cost on our end to pass on to you.
Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. The conversion happens entirely on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your files are never sent to Nexsia or any third party — closing the tab is the same as deleting them.
Is there a file size or file count limit?
No hard limit, though very large batches (dozens of high-resolution photos) will take longer since your device is doing the work instead of a server. For most use cases — a handful of scans or photos — conversion is near-instant.
Does this work on my phone?
Yes — you can select photos directly from your phone's camera roll, reorder them, and convert, all from the mobile browser.
What image formats are supported?
JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP. If you need to convert a HEIC file (the default format on iPhones), convert it to JPG first — iPhones typically do this automatically when you share or export a photo to another app.
