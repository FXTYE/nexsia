Common questions

Kling AI vs Veo 3 FAQs

Which is better, Kling AI or Veo 3?
Depends on your priority. Kling wins on price and camera-control tools; Veo wins on native audio and realism. For high-volume, budget-conscious work, Kling is stronger. For projects needing dialogue or sound, Veo is worth the premium.
Which is cheaper?
Kling AI. Its entry plan starts at $10/mo versus $19.99/mo for Google AI Pro, and its per-second generation cost (~$0.10) runs well under Veo 3's (~$0.15-0.40).
Does Kling AI generate audio?
No. Kling clips are silent by default. Veo 3 is the one generating dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio natively, synced to the video.
Which has better camera controls?
Kling AI's dolly, pan and orbit controls are more explicit and directly directable than Veo 3's, which relies on Flow's scene-building tools for similar effects.
Can I use both?
Yes — many creators use Kling for high-volume, budget-conscious generation and switch to Veo 3 specifically when a shot needs dialogue or synced sound effects.
