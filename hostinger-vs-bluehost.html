Our verdict

This comparison has a fairly clear lean, but real trade-offs on both sides. Common questions

Hostinger vs Bluehost FAQs

Is Hostinger or Bluehost better?
Hostinger has a substantially better review track record (4.7★ vs 3.2★ on Trustpilot) and more websites on its entry plan. Bluehost has the official WordPress.org endorsement and a slightly lower renewal price. For most people, Hostinger's review consistency makes it the safer recommendation.
Which is cheaper?
Bluehost's renewal price is slightly lower ($9.99/mo vs $10.99/mo for the entry tier), though Hostinger's intro price is comparable and includes more websites (3 vs 1). The difference is small either way — the bigger factor is the review-trust gap.
Is Bluehost really recommended by WordPress.org?
Yes — Bluehost is one of a small number of hosts WordPress.org names directly as a recommended provider. Hostinger has no equivalent official endorsement, though it performs comparably well in independent testing.
Why is Bluehost's Trustpilot score so much lower?
Reviews point to domain redemption fees and checkout upsells as the main drivers — a real, consistent pattern across a large 29,000+ review sample, not isolated complaints. Hostinger doesn't share this specific pattern.
Can I switch from Bluehost to Hostinger later?
Yes, though you should check Bluehost's domain redemption policy first if you registered your domain through them — some reviewers report unexpectedly high fees when leaving. Hostinger provides migration tools to help move your site over.
