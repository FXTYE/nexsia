Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how to reach us about it.

This explains what information Nexsia collects when you visit, why, and what control you have over it. We've kept it in plain English rather than dense legal text.

Last updated August 2026

What we collect

  • Basic analytics — pages visited, general location (country/city level), device type and referring site, collected in aggregate so we can see what's useful and what isn't.
  • Affiliate click tracking — when you click a "Try [tool]" button, a cookie tags that click so the company you're sent to knows the referral came from Nexsia. This is how we earn commissions — see our affiliate disclosure for the full picture.
  • Contact form submissions — if you use our contact page, we collect your name, email address, and whatever you write to us, solely to respond to you.
  • Nothing else. We don't require accounts, we don't sell personal data, and we don't run invasive tracking or ad-retargeting pixels.

Cookies

Nexsia uses a small number of cookies: affiliate-tracking cookies (set by the tool you clicked through to, not by us directly) and basic analytics cookies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site will still work, though affiliate links won't be properly attributed if you do.

Third parties

Affiliate networks

When you click through to a tool we review, that company's own privacy policy governs what happens next — we have no visibility into or control over what they do with your data once you land on their site.

Analytics providers

We use standard, privacy-respecting analytics to understand site traffic in aggregate. We don't sell or share this data with advertisers.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, request it be deleted, or ask a question about any of this — contact us and we'll respond directly. Since we don't require accounts and collect very little to begin with, most requests are simple to resolve.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "last updated" date above. We won't retroactively use data in a way this policy doesn't already describe.

Questions about any of this? Get in touch — we're happy to explain.
