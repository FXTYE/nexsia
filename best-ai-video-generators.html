Common questions

Best AI video generators — FAQs

What's the best AI video generator overall?
Veo 3 is our overall pick for 2026 — it's the only major model generating synced dialogue and sound effects natively, alongside best-in-class realism. Kling AI is the better pick if price matters more than audio.
Is Sora still worth using?
Its output quality still holds up, but the standalone Sora app was discontinued in April 2026 and the Sora 2 API is being sunset on September 24, 2026. Access now requires ChatGPT Plus or Pro, with no free tier. For a new project, we'd recommend Veo 3 or Kling AI instead.
Which AI video tool is cheapest?
Kling AI has the best access economics in the category at roughly $0.10 per generated second — well under Veo, Runway or Sora's former API pricing.
Which tool generates audio with the video?
Veo 3 is currently the only major model generating dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio natively in sync with the picture. Kling, Runway, Higgsfield's underlying models and Sora all require a separate audio pass.
Which is best for professional video editing workflows?
Runway, by a clear margin — it's the only platform here built around real timeline and editing tools rather than generation alone, which is why it's widely used in professional and agency production pipelines.
