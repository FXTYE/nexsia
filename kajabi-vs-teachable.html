Our verdict

This comes down to your revenue and how many separate tools you're already paying for. Teachable is genuinely cheaper below roughly $1,520/month in course sales, comes with native mobile apps on every plan, and handles your sales tax and VAT automatically — real, underrated advantages. Above that revenue line, its 7.5% transaction fee starts costing more than Kajabi's flat subscription.

The honest test: do the math on your actual revenue, not the sticker price. If you're pre-revenue or just validating an idea, Teachable's lower entry cost and simplicity make sense. If you're an established course creator who's also paying for email marketing and landing page tools separately, Kajabi likely costs less in total than the stack you're already running.

Common questions

Kajabi vs Teachable FAQs

Is Kajabi or Teachable better?
Neither is 'better' outright — it depends on your revenue and whether you want marketing tools included. Teachable is cheaper below ~$1,520/month in course sales; Kajabi wins above that once you account for its 0% transaction fees and included marketing tools.
Which is cheaper?
It depends on your revenue. Teachable's entry plan (~$29-39/mo) is cheaper upfront, but its 7.5% transaction fee can cost more than Kajabi's flat subscription once you're selling consistently — the crossover is around $1,520/month.
Does Kajabi charge transaction fees?
No — Kajabi charges 0% transaction fees on all plans. It does add a small surcharge (0.5-2%) if you connect your own Stripe account instead of using Kajabi Payments.
Does Teachable handle sales tax?
Yes — Teachable acts as Merchant of Record, automatically handling US sales tax, EU VAT and GST across regions. Kajabi does not do this; you're responsible for your own tax compliance.
Can I switch from Teachable to Kajabi later?
Yes, though migrating course content, students and payment history takes manual work on both platforms — there's no official one-click migration tool between them.
