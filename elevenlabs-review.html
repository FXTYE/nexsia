Common questions

ElevenLabs FAQs

Is ElevenLabs free?
Yes — the free plan gives 10,000 characters a month and 3 custom voices, enough to test quality. It's personal-use only; commercial rights start on the paid Starter plan (around $5/month).
Is ElevenLabs good for YouTube?
Very. It's the most natural AI voice available, with dubbing to reach other languages — ideal for narration and faceless channels. Watch character limits on cheaper tiers.
ElevenLabs vs Murf — which is better?
For creators, ElevenLabs wins on realism, cloning and price. Murf suits teams producing narrated presentations. See our full ElevenLabs vs Murf comparison.
Can I clone my own voice?
Yes, on Starter and above, and it's among the most accurate cloning available. Only clone voices you have permission to use.
Do I get commercial rights?
Paid plans (Starter and up) include a commercial license. The free plan is personal, non-commercial use only.
