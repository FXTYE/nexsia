Common questions

Hostinger vs SiteGround FAQs

Is Hostinger or SiteGround better?
Neither is 'better' outright — they're built for different priorities. Hostinger is better if price is your main constraint; SiteGround is better if support quality and infrastructure reliability matter more than the lowest cost.
Which is cheaper?
Hostinger is cheaper at every tier, both intro and renewal — Premium renews at $10.99/mo versus SiteGround StartUp's $14.99-24.99/mo depending on term. The gap is real and consistent, not just a promotional difference.
Does SiteGround really perform better than Hostinger?
Independent migration data shows sites moved to SiteGround from other hosts load meaningfully faster on average, likely due to Google Cloud infrastructure. Both hosts perform well for typical sites, but SiteGround has a measurable edge in benchmarks.
Why is SiteGround's renewal price different for different people?
SiteGround's renewal rate depends on which billing term you originally chose — 24-month terms renew lower than 12-month or month-to-month terms. Hostinger's renewal rate is fixed regardless of your original term.
Can I switch from Hostinger to SiteGround later?
Yes — SiteGround offers free migration for new customers moving from other hosts, making the switch easier than starting from scratch. Hostinger also provides migration tools, though the process is more self-service.
