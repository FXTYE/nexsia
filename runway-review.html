Common questions

Runway FAQs

Is Runway free?
There's a one-time free allowance of 125 credits to try the platform — not an ongoing free plan. At 12 credits per second on Gen-4.5, that covers a short evaluation, not regular use. Paid plans start at $12/mo (Standard, annual).
What makes Runway different from Veo or Kling?
Runway is built around editing, not just generation — it's the only major platform in this category with real timeline tools designed for integrating AI clips into a production workflow. Veo and Kling focus on generation quality and price instead.
Runway vs Veo 3 — which is better?
Runway wins if editing tools and production workflow matter to you. Veo wins on native audio and realism. Many professional editors use Runway specifically because nothing else in the category offers comparable editing tools.
Why did Runway's pricing change?
Runway restructured its plans in 2026 — old Standard became Pro, old Pro became Max, and a new entry Standard tier was introduced. Older articles online may still reference the previous names and credit allowances.
