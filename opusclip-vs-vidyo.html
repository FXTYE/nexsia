In depth

Pros & cons of each platform

O OpusClip

Pro

Most developed virality scoring

The virality score is a genuinely useful signal for prioritizing which clips to post first, and it's more refined than Vidyo.ai's version of the same idea.

Pro

Auto B-roll and stronger caption animation

Extra polish — automatic B-roll insertion and more developed caption styling — gets clips closer to publish-ready without a manual edit pass.

Con

Charges by source length, not output

Credits are consumed by the length of video you upload, not how many clips you actually use — upload a 30-minute podcast and it costs 30 credits whether you use 1 clip or 15.

Con

Editor is gated on lower tiers

On Free and Starter ($15/mo), clips can't be edited at all — you get exactly what the AI produced, mistakes included. Editing tools require the higher tier.

V Vidyo.ai

Pro

Stronger for B2B and LinkedIn content

Vidyo.ai leans into thought-leadership and webinar repurposing, with a workflow and interface that feels more tailored to professional/B2B creators than OpusClip's.

Pro

Better multi-language support

For non-English or multi-language content, Vidyo.ai is consistently cited as the stronger choice over OpusClip.

Con

Less developed virality scoring

Carries a virality-prediction feature similar to OpusClip's, but it's generally considered less refined and useful for prioritizing clips.

Con

No auto B-roll

Clips come out less "finished" than OpusClip's — expect more manual polish before publishing if visual variety matters to you.

Both tools require source footage to work with — neither helps if you haven't filmed anything. Your short-form output stays tied to your long-form production schedule either way.

Common questions

OpusClip vs Vidyo.ai FAQs

Which makes better clips?
OpusClip, in our testing. It selects the most clippable moments more reliably and ranks them by a virality score, plus adds auto B-roll. Vidyo.ai clips well too, but with less polish.
Which is cheaper?
Vidyo.ai is often the cheaper starting point, especially during promotions. Both have free plans, so you can compare output on the same video before paying.
Do the clips need editing after?
Usually a quick pass on either. Caption timing and clip start/end points benefit from a light human touch — OpusClip tends to land closer to publish-ready thanks to B-roll and cleaner reframing.
Can I use either for TikTok and Reels too?
Yes. Both export vertical 9:16 clips suitable for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, with captions baked in.
Which should a beginner choose?
If budget is tight and you're just testing short-form, start with Vidyo.ai's free tier. If you're serious about a Shorts pipeline, OpusClip's quality pays off sooner.
