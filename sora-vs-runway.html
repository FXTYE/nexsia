Common questions

Sora vs Runway FAQs

Is Sora or Runway better?
They serve different needs. Sora edges Runway on raw photorealism but offers no editing tools and is winding down as a standalone product. Runway pairs strong generation with a real editing timeline and is actively developed — the more practical choice for most projects.
Can I edit videos with Sora?
No — Sora produces a raw generation with no built-in editing tools. Runway is built specifically around editing what you generate, with a real timeline and production tooling.
Is Sora still available?
Only through ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Pro ($200/mo). The standalone Sora app was discontinued in April 2026, and its API is being sunset on September 24, 2026.
Which is cheaper?
Runway's Standard tier starts at $12/mo (annual), below the $20/mo ChatGPT Plus subscription now required for any Sora access.
