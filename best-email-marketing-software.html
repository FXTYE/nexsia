Common questions

Best email marketing software — FAQs

What's the best email marketing software overall?
There's no single winner — it depends on your business stage. Mailchimp is the pick for beginners who want the easiest start, Kit for creators who want a free tier and built-in monetization, and ActiveCampaign for anyone who needs deep automation and a built-in CRM. Match the tool to the job, not the hype.
Which email tool has the best free plan?
Kit's free plan supports up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited sends, which is genuinely unmatched in this category. Mailchimp's free plan is smaller (250 contacts as of 2026) but includes a template library. ActiveCampaign has no free plan at all — just a 14-day trial.
Do I need a CRM alongside my email tool?
If you're managing a sales process with multiple stages — leads, deals, follow-ups — a built-in CRM like ActiveCampaign's saves you from paying for and connecting a separate tool. If you're mainly sending newsletters or campaigns, Mailchimp or Kit's simpler feature sets are usually enough.
Why do these tools have such different Trustpilot scores?
Across this category, Trustpilot scores tend to reflect billing and support experiences more than the product itself — Mailchimp (2.9★) and ActiveCampaign (2.8★) both score far below their G2 ratings (4.3-4.5★) for this reason. Read the specific complaints, not just the star rating, before deciding.
How do you rank these tools?
We test each tool hands-on and score it on ease of use, price and automation depth for its category, then cross-check against public sentiment from G2, Trustpilot and Reddit. We disclose affiliate links and are upfront about each tool's real weaknesses.
