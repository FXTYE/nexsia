Common questions

Sora vs Veo 3 FAQs

Is Sora better than Veo 3?
On raw photorealism, Sora holds a narrow edge in some tests. But Veo 3 adds native audio generation and is actively developed with multiple access paths, while Sora's standalone app is discontinued and its API is sunsetting September 24, 2026 — making Veo the more practical choice for most people.
Can I still use Sora?
Yes, through a ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Pro ($200/mo) subscription — but there's no dedicated Sora app anymore, no free tier, and the underlying API is being wound down.
Which is cheaper?
Entry pricing is close ($19.99/mo for Google AI Pro vs $20/mo for ChatGPT Plus), but Veo 3 also offers a genuine free tier with daily credits via Flow, which Sora no longer has.
Does Sora generate sound?
No. Sora clips are silent by default. Veo 3 is the one generating dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio natively, synced to the picture.
Should I build a product on Sora or Veo 3?
Veo 3. Sora's API is confirmed to stop accepting requests on September 24, 2026, which makes it unsuitable for anything you plan to maintain. Veo 3 is available via Vertex AI for ongoing developer use.
