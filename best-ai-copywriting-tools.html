Common questions

Best AI copywriting & ad tools — FAQs

What's the best AI copywriting tool overall?
There's no single winner — it depends on what you're producing. Jasper is the pick for polished long-form writing, Copy.ai for automating repeatable marketing workflows at scale, and AdCreative.ai for generating ad-specific visuals and copy fast. Match the tool to the job, not the hype.
Is Copy.ai still a copywriting tool?
Not primarily — it repositioned in 2024 into a "Go-To-Market AI" platform with sales prospecting and CRM features. The entry Chat plan still works for basic writing, but the automation that defines the product now sits behind pricing starting around $1,000/month.
Do I need a separate tool for ad creatives?
If you're running paid campaigns needing many visual variants fast, yes — AdCreative.ai's Creative Scoring gives you a data-driven starting point that general writing tools don't offer. For occasional ad copy, Jasper or Copy.ai's templates may be enough.
Is there a free option for AI copywriting?
Copy.ai has a genuine free plan (2,000 words/month), and free general chatbots like ChatGPT or Claude handle plenty of simple writing tasks. Jasper and AdCreative.ai offer free trials rather than permanent free tiers.
How do you rank these tools?
We test each tool hands-on and score it on output quality, price and learning curve for its category, then cross-check against public sentiment from G2, Trustpilot and Reddit. We disclose affiliate links and are upfront about each tool's real weaknesses.
