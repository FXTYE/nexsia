Common questions

Tidio vs Intercom FAQs

Tidio vs Intercom — which is better?
Tidio wins on price and setup speed for small teams; Intercom wins on AI quality and messaging polish for businesses that can afford the premium. They serve very different budgets.
Is Tidio a good Intercom alternative?
For a small store or site, yes — it covers the core live chat use case at a fraction of the cost, though Lyro AI is noticeably less capable than Intercom's Fin.
Which has better AI?
Intercom's Fin, by a clear margin — it handles more complex, multi-step conversations better than Tidio's more basic Lyro chatbot.
Is Intercom worth the price jump over Tidio?
If you're a product-led SaaS business that needs proactive messaging, onboarding tours, and top-tier AI resolution quality, yes. For a simple live chat widget, Tidio covers the basics for much less.
Which is cheaper for a small team?
Tidio, by a wide margin — its Starter plan at $29/mo is a fraction of Intercom's $19–29/agent/mo, especially once you factor in multiple agents.
