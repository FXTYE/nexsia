What's the best VPN overall?
NordVPN is our overall pick for 2026 — it's the fastest VPN we tested in independent benchmarks and offers the largest server network of the five compared here, backed by genuinely useful extras like Threat Protection. If you need unlimited devices instead, Surfshark is the stronger pick.
Is there a genuinely free VPN worth using?
Yes — ProtonVPN's free plan has no data cap or time limit, and its no-logs policy has been independently audited multiple times, unlike most "free" VPN trials. It's limited to one device and fewer server locations, but it's the one free option we'd actually recommend.
Which VPN is cheapest?
CyberGhost has the lowest starting price at $2.19/mo on its long-term plan, followed closely by Surfshark at $2.49/mo. ProtonVPN's free tier is the only genuinely free option in this comparison.
Which VPN allows the most devices?
Surfshark, with unlimited simultaneous device connections on every plan — no other provider in this comparison matches that.
Which VPN is fastest?
NordVPN, hitting 857 Mbps in independent 2026 speed testing, ahead of ExpressVPN's 808 Mbps and the rest of the category.
Which VPN has the most servers?
CyberGhost, with 11,000+ servers across 100 countries, ahead of NordVPN's 9,300+ across 137 countries. NordVPN's network covers more countries despite the smaller total server count.
