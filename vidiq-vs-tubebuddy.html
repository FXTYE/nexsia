Common questions

vidIQ vs TubeBuddy FAQs

Is vidIQ or TubeBuddy better for beginners?
TubeBuddy is the gentler start — it's cheaper, and channels under 1,000 subscribers get roughly 50% off lower tiers. vidIQ's free plan is also a solid way to learn keyword research at no cost. Many beginners run both free plans before paying for either.
Can I use vidIQ and TubeBuddy together?
Yes, and it's a common setup. Creators often run vidIQ's free plan for keyword research and ideas while paying for TubeBuddy's A/B testing and bulk editing. The two extensions coexist fine in the same browser.
Which is cheaper?
TubeBuddy, at both ends. Its Pro plan starts around $4.99/month (~$3 billed annually) versus vidIQ Boost at ~$16.58/month annually, and TubeBuddy's top Legend tier also undercuts vidIQ's Max.
Does vidIQ have A/B testing?
No. Thumbnail and title A/B testing is one of TubeBuddy's key differentiators — vidIQ doesn't offer it at any tier. If split-testing is central to your strategy, that alone can decide it.
Are these tools safe for my YouTube channel?
Yes. Both are official YouTube partner tools that use the YouTube API, and are trusted by millions of creators. Neither violates YouTube's terms.
