The quick verdict

ClickFunnels is still the strongest dedicated funnel builder on the market — the page editor, 2-step checkout, native upsells and Payments AI are genuinely best-in-class for turning paid traffic into sales. If your business lives and dies by a sales funnel, nothing else matches its polish.

But it has real weaknesses it won't advertise: the blog and SEO tools don't compete with WordPress, the built-in CRM is thin next to a real CRM, email costs climb as your list grows, and the in-app upselling is relentless. If you need more than funnels — a full CRM, SMS, or an agency-resale model — GoHighLevel does more for similar money. If your business is courses and community, Kajabi fits better.

Know before you buy

The ClickFunnels 2.0 migration, honestly

This is the one thing most reviews gloss over, and it genuinely affects whether ClickFunnels is a safe buy today. In 2022, ClickFunnels rebuilt the platform from scratch as "2.0," adding courses, community, a blog and email marketing on top of the original funnel builder. The rebuild took years to stabilize, and it shows in how the userbase is split.

New signups are placed on 2.0 — existing users often stay on Classic

Long-time customers frequently choose to remain on the original "Classic" version for stability, because 2.0 has a history of bugs from the transition. If you're signing up today you'll be on 2.0 by default, so it's worth reading recent (not 2022-era) reviews specifically about 2.0's current stability before committing budget to a complex funnel build.

The practical upside of 2.0: it's the version with the courses, blog, CRM and email features — so if you want the "all-in-one" pitch, 2.0 is what delivers it, imperfectly. If you only want the pure funnel builder and checkout, that part of 2.0 is considered strong and stable.

Common questions

ClickFunnels FAQs

Is ClickFunnels worth it in 2026?
Yes, if your business runs primarily on sales funnels and you value the best-in-class checkout and upsell mechanics. It's a weaker fit if you need a real CRM, strong SEO tools, or you're price-sensitive — in those cases GoHighLevel, Kajabi, or Systeme.io are worth comparing first.
What's the difference between ClickFunnels Classic and 2.0?
2.0 is a ground-up rebuild adding courses, a blog, CRM and email on top of the original funnel builder. New signups default to 2.0. Some long-time users stayed on Classic for stability during 2.0's rocky early years — worth checking current, not historical, reviews on 2.0's stability.
How much does ClickFunnels really cost?
Startup runs ~$97/mo and Pro ~$297/mo (both save about 17% on annual billing). Email is billed per-send on top and increases as your contact list grows — factor that into the real monthly cost at scale.
ClickFunnels vs GoHighLevel — which is better?
ClickFunnels has the more polished funnel builder; GoHighLevel bundles a full CRM, SMS and white-label resale for similar entry pricing. Agencies and anyone wanting more than funnels tend to prefer GoHighLevel. See our full comparison.
ClickFunnels vs Kajabi — which is better?
They're not really direct competitors — ClickFunnels is built for funnels, Kajabi for courses and community. Pick based on what your business actually sells. See our full comparison.
Is there a free version of ClickFunnels?
No permanent free plan — there's a 14-day free trial that requires a credit card. If you want a genuinely free entry point, Systeme.io is the closest alternative with a real free tier.
