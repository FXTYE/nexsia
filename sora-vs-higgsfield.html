Common questions

Sora vs Higgsfield FAQs

Can I access Sora through Higgsfield?
Higgsfield bundles access to several underlying models including Sora, Veo, Kling and Seedance under one dashboard — so yes, in many cases you can generate with Sora's model through Higgsfield rather than a direct ChatGPT subscription.
Is Sora still available directly?
Only through ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Pro ($200/mo). The standalone Sora app was discontinued in April 2026, and its API is being sunset on September 24, 2026.
What is Higgsfield's Soul ID?
A character-consistency system — upload a few reference photos, train a reusable character, and keep that face consistent across generations. Sora has no equivalent built-in tool.
Which is cheaper?
Higgsfield's entry Basic plan is $9/mo (annual), below the $20/mo ChatGPT Plus subscription now required for any direct Sora access.
