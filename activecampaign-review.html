The quick verdict

ActiveCampaign's automation engine is legitimately the best in this category — branching workflows, predictive sending, and a built-in CRM give it capability that platforms like Mailchimp and Kit simply don't attempt, and G2's 14,000+ reviews back that up consistently.

But the customer experience outside the product itself is a real concern. Trustpilot's 2.8★ rating is driven by billing disputes, cancellation difficulty, and a support system users increasingly describe as automated-first — and since November 3, 2025, new accounts are billed for every contact including unsubscribes and hard bounces, quietly raising real costs. If you need serious automation and are comfortable managing your account carefully, the product delivers — go in with clear eyes about the account-side friction.

Know before you buy

The November 2025 billing change, honestly

This is the detail that changes real budgets and doesn't get enough attention. As of November 3, 2025, ActiveCampaign changed how it counts contacts for new accounts — a change that quietly increases what many businesses actually pay, independent of any plan upgrade.

New accounts now pay for unsubscribed and bounced contacts too

Previously, ActiveCampaign only billed for active contacts. For accounts created on or after November 3, 2025, pricing counts every contact regardless of status — including unsubscribes and hard bounces. Accounts created before that date keep the old active-contacts-only pricing, so the impact depends entirely on when you signed up.

Two more costs worth budgeting for: the roughly 20% annual-billing discount only applies if you prepay a full year, and CRM features, landing pages, and lead scoring are gated behind the Plus plan and up — the Starter plan's $15/month headline price doesn't include them.

Common questions

ActiveCampaign FAQs

Is ActiveCampaign worth it in 2026?
Yes, if you need genuinely complex automation or want a built-in CRM without a separate tool — the product itself is rated highly and consistently (G2: 4.4-4.5★, 14,000+ reviews). It's a harder recommendation if you're billing-sensitive, since Trustpilot's 2.8★ reflects real, recurring account and support friction.
Why is ActiveCampaign's Trustpilot rating so low?
The low score is driven almost entirely by billing disputes, cancellation difficulty, and slow support access — not the automation engine itself, which is consistently praised on G2. The split mirrors a pattern seen across this category: feature-focused review sites rate the product; Trustpilot skews toward people who had an account problem.
What changed with ActiveCampaign's billing in November 2025?
As of November 3, 2025, new accounts are billed for every contact regardless of status — including unsubscribed and hard-bounced contacts. Accounts created before that date keep the previous active-contacts-only pricing. This can meaningfully raise your real bill without any change to your actual sending.
ActiveCampaign vs Mailchimp — which is better?
ActiveCampaign has meaningfully deeper automation and a built-in CRM; Mailchimp is easier to learn and has a larger template library. If you're past basic broadcast emails and need real workflow logic, ActiveCampaign is usually the better long-term fit. See our full comparison.
Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?
No — there's a 14-day free trial on all paid tiers, but no permanent free plan. If a genuine free tier matters to you, Kit's 10,000-subscriber free plan is the closest comparable option in this category.
Do I need the CRM, or just email?
If you only need email and light automation, the Starter plan ($15/mo) covers that — but CRM, landing pages, and lead scoring are all gated behind Plus ($49/mo) and up. Most businesses that need ActiveCampaign at all end up on Plus once they actually use the platform.
