    Common questions

    Word Counter FAQs

    Is this really free?
    Yes — there's no signup, no watermark and no limit on how much text you can count. It runs entirely in your browser, so there's no server cost on our end to pass on to you.
    Is my text uploaded anywhere?
    No. Counting happens entirely on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your text is never sent to Nexsia or any third party — closing the tab is the same as deleting it.
    How is reading time calculated?
    We use an average silent reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time assumes an average speaking pace of 130 words per minute — both are estimates and will vary by reader or speaker.
    How are sentences and paragraphs counted?
    Sentences are counted by splitting on ., ! and ? followed by a space or line end. Paragraphs are counted as blocks of text separated by a blank line. Unusual punctuation or formatting can affect accuracy slightly.
    Does this work for languages other than English?
    Word and character counts work for any language using spaces between words. Character count is always accurate; word/sentence splitting is tuned for space-separated languages and may be less precise for languages like Chinese or Japanese that don't use spaces.
    Is there a character limit?
    No hard limit — the tool has been tested with very long documents. Extremely large pastes (hundreds of thousands of words) may feel slightly less instant since your device is doing all the processing.
