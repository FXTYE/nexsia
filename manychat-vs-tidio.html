Common questions

ManyChat vs Tidio FAQs

ManyChat vs Tidio — which should I use?
It depends on the job: ManyChat for Instagram/WhatsApp/Messenger DM automation and social marketing, Tidio for a website live chat widget. Many businesses could reasonably use both for different purposes.
Can ManyChat replace a website chat widget?
Not really — its strength is social and messaging channel automation, not embedding a chat bubble on your website the way Tidio does.
Can Tidio replace ManyChat for Instagram marketing?
No — Tidio has no comment-to-DM automation or the broader social campaign tooling that defines ManyChat's core product.
Which is cheaper?
ManyChat's Essential tier at $17/mo undercuts Tidio's Starter at $29/mo, though both offer free plans with real limitations.
Do both have AI chatbots?
Tidio's Lyro is purpose-built for FAQ-style support conversations (as a separate add-on); ManyChat's flow builder handles automation but isn't a dedicated support AI in the same sense.
