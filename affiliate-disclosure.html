Affiliate Disclosure

Plain-English: how this site makes money.

This is the plain-English version of how Nexsia makes money. No fine print, no burying it — the FTC requires disclosure, and honestly, you deserve to know anyway.

The short version

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up for a paid plan, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. You pay the same price you'd pay going direct.

How it works

  • We join affiliate programs run by tools we review (through networks like PartnerStack, Impact and Rewardful).
  • When we link to those tools, the link is tagged so the company knows the referral came from us.
  • If you subscribe, they pay us a percentage — often a recurring one — out of their own margin.
  • Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored" and typically say "Try [tool]" on the button.

What this does — and doesn't — change

It does not affect our ratings

We score and rank tools on merit, using our review methodology. A higher commission never buys a better score or position. We frequently recommend free tools (like ChatGPT, Claude or Canva) that pay us nothing, because they're sometimes the best answer.

It keeps the site running

Commissions are how an independent site like this stays free to read and free of paid placements. If our recommendations weren't honest, you'd stop trusting them — so being straight with you is also just good business.

Not every link pays us

Plenty of tools we cover either don't have an affiliate program or aren't accepting new partners. We review and recommend them the same way regardless. Our job is to point you at the right tool — whether or not there's a commission attached.
