The quick verdict

Zendesk earns its spot as the enterprise benchmark because nothing else in this category matches its depth of customization, reporting, and marketplace integrations. The 2026 shift to outcome-based AI pricing is a genuine improvement for teams with unpredictable ticket volume, but it also means your AI bill is harder to forecast than a flat per-seat competitor like Intercom. If you need enterprise-grade flexibility and don't mind assembling your final price from several line items, Zendesk is still the safest default.

Common questions

Zendesk FAQs

Is Zendesk free?
No — there's no permanent free plan, only a free trial. Support Team starts at $19/agent/mo on annual billing.
How much does Zendesk AI actually cost?
Basic AI deflection (Essential) is included in every Suite plan. The more powerful Advanced AI Agent is billed separately at roughly $2 per automated resolution pay-as-you-go, or about $1.50 with a committed volume.
Zendesk vs Intercom — which is better?
Zendesk wins on customization, reporting depth and its app marketplace; Intercom wins on live chat and in-app messaging with more predictable per-resolution AI pricing via Fin. Larger, complex operations tend to favor Zendesk.
Is Zendesk hard to set up?
It has a steeper learning curve than Freshdesk or Tidio — most of the negative sentiment around Zendesk is about setup complexity, not the platform's capability once configured.
Does Zendesk work well for ecommerce?
It can, but Gorgias is the more purpose-built option for Shopify stores specifically, with order data surfaced directly inside every ticket.
