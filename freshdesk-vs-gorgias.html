Our verdict

For ecommerce and Shopify stores specifically, Gorgias is the clear winner. Its native order-data integration and unlimited agent seats aren't something Freshdesk can replicate without third-party apps.

Freshdesk wins for any business outside pure ecommerce, especially those that need phone support as a core channel — its broader industry fit and predictable per-seat pricing make it the safer general-purpose choice.
