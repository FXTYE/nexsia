Common questions

TubeBuddy FAQs

Is TubeBuddy free?
Yes, TubeBuddy has a free plan with core tools. Paid plans start around $4.99/month (~$3 billed annually), and channels under 1,000 subscribers get roughly 50% off the lower tiers.
Is TubeBuddy worth it in 2026?
For optimising an existing channel, yes — the A/B testing, bulk tools and retention analysis are genuinely useful and cheaper than vidIQ. If you mainly need deep keyword research, vidIQ may suit you better.
TubeBuddy vs vidIQ — which is better?
TubeBuddy wins on price, A/B testing and bulk tools; vidIQ wins on keyword research and AI ideation. Many creators run vidIQ free for research alongside a paid TubeBuddy plan.
Do I need 1,000 subscribers for A/B testing?
Yes. TubeBuddy's thumbnail and title A/B testing requires a channel with at least 1,000 subscribers, so the smallest channels can't use that feature yet.
Is TubeBuddy safe for my channel?
Yes. TubeBuddy is an official YouTube partner tool that uses the YouTube API and is trusted by millions of creators. It doesn't violate YouTube's terms.
