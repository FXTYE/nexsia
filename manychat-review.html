Common questions

ManyChat FAQs

Is ManyChat free?
Yes — the Free plan supports up to 25 active contacts per month, though most real campaigns will outgrow it quickly and need a paid tier.
How much does ManyChat really cost?
Real-world spend ranges from roughly $100/month for a lean, single-channel setup to well over $1,000/month for a business running WhatsApp, SMS and AI at scale, once messaging and overage fees are included.
Is ManyChat a helpdesk replacement?
Not really — it's built for social and messaging marketing automation, not traditional ticket-based support. For real helpdesk needs, Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom are the better fit.
ManyChat vs Tidio — which is better?
ManyChat wins decisively for Instagram/WhatsApp DM automation and marketing flows; Tidio wins if you specifically need a website live chat widget. They solve different problems more than they compete directly.
Does ManyChat's pricing change often?
Yes — ManyChat overhauled its entire pricing structure in March 2026, replacing the old Free/Pro system with five tiers and adding per-contact overage fees across the board.
