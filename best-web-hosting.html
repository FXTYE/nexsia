Common questions

Best web hosting — FAQs

What's the best web hosting overall?
There's no single winner — it depends on what you're optimizing for. Hostinger is the pick for the lowest price and the best review track record, SiteGround for support quality and Google Cloud infrastructure, and Bluehost for the official WordPress.org endorsement at the lowest renewal price. Match the host to your priority, not the hype.
Which web host has the best renewal pricing?
Bluehost's renewal is the lowest of the three at $9.99/mo for Basic, just ahead of Hostinger's $10.99/mo for Premium. SiteGround's renewal is meaningfully higher ($14.99-24.99/mo depending on original term) but reflects its stronger support and infrastructure.
Why do these hosts have such different Trustpilot scores?
Hostinger and SiteGround both score 4.7★, while Bluehost trails at 3.2★ — a real, well-documented gap driven by domain redemption fees and checkout upsell complaints specific to Bluehost. Read the specific complaints, not just the star rating, before deciding.
Do I need Google Cloud infrastructure for my site?
Most small sites run fine on standard shared hosting from Hostinger or Bluehost. SiteGround's Google Cloud backing shows up as a measurable speed advantage in independent benchmarks, which matters more for business-critical sites where every second of load time affects conversions.
How do you rank these hosts?
We research real 2026 pricing including renewal rates (not just intro prices), cross-check independent review platforms like G2 and Trustpilot, and weigh documented complaint patterns from multiple sources. We disclose affiliate links and are upfront about each host's real weaknesses.
