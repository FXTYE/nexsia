Common questions

Best sales funnel software — FAQs

What's the best sales funnel software overall?
There's no single winner — it depends on what your business actually sells. ClickFunnels is the pick for pure sales funnels, GoHighLevel for agencies wanting a full CRM and white-label resale, and Kajabi for courses and community. Match the tool to your business model, not the other way around.
What's the difference between a funnel builder and an all-in-one platform?
A funnel builder like ClickFunnels focuses on landing pages, checkouts and upsells. An all-in-one like GoHighLevel adds a full CRM, SMS, email, calendars and reputation management — replacing several separate tools, at the cost of a steeper learning curve.
Do I need a course platform if I'm not selling courses yet?
No — start with the platform that matches what you're selling today. ClickFunnels or GoHighLevel can host basic courses if you add them later; a dedicated platform like Kajabi only pays off once course delivery and student experience are central to your business.
Is there a free option for sales funnel software?
None of the three platforms here has a permanent free plan, though all offer free trials. Systeme.io is the closest genuinely free alternative if you want to test the funnel-building model before paying anything.
