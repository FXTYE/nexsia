Review Methodology

How we test, score and review every tool on this site.

Nexsia exists to help people pick the right software without wasting money — whether that's a creator tool, a sales funnel, a course platform or a copywriting tool. Every review and comparison on this site follows the same process. Here's exactly how we work — so you can judge whether to trust our verdicts.

How we score tools

Each tool is rated out of 5 on four things that actually matter when you're the one paying for it:

  • Output quality — how good the result is, and how much cleanup or setup it needs before it's genuinely useful.
  • Speed & workflow — how much time or friction it genuinely saves in a real day-to-day workflow.
  • Price & value — what you get for the money, including how usable the free tier or trial is.
  • Learning curve — how quickly a normal user can get useful results without a manual.

We don't give everything 4.8 stars. A believable range of scores is the whole point — if every tool scored top marks, the ratings would be worthless.

Our research process

1Hands-on testing where we can

We use tools directly on real tasks — clipping a long video, building a funnel step, drafting a script, generating a voiceover, checking a course platform's checkout flow — rather than reading a feature list.

2Cross-checking public sentiment

We compare our experience against verified reviews on G2, Trustpilot and Capterra, plus real discussion on Reddit and YouTube. Where we cite sentiment, we link the source.

3Verifying pricing at the source

Prices change constantly. We check each tool's own pricing page and date every review, and we revisit them regularly to keep numbers current.

4Writing the honest verdict

We say who a tool is not for, not just who it's for — and we name free or non-affiliate tools when they're genuinely the best answer.

Our promise on independence

  • We earn affiliate commissions from some (not all) tools we recommend — this never changes a score or ranking.
  • No company pays us for a review, a position, or a higher rating. We don't accept paid placements.
  • When the best tool for a job is free or doesn't pay us a commission, we recommend it anyway.
  • We update reviews as tools change, and we date every one so you know how current it is.
