Common questions

ExpressVPN FAQs

Is ExpressVPN free?
No — there's no free tier, only a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. ProtonVPN is the better option if you want a genuinely free VPN to try first.
What are ExpressVPN's new Basic, Advanced and Pro tiers?
A 2026 pricing overhaul that replaces the old single-price model. Basic covers core VPN access on 10 devices, Advanced adds more devices and a password manager, and Pro adds a dedicated IP and the most device slots.
ExpressVPN vs NordVPN — which is better?
NordVPN wins on raw speed and server network size; ExpressVPN wins on app polish, ease of use and Lightway's fast reconnects. If you want the simplest experience, go ExpressVPN. If you want the fastest speeds and biggest server list, go NordVPN.
Does ExpressVPN work with Netflix?
Yes — ExpressVPN is consistently one of the more reliable VPNs for unblocking major streaming platforms across regions.
How many devices can I use with one ExpressVPN account?
Between 10 and 14, depending on which of the three 2026 tiers you choose. If you need unlimited devices, Surfshark is the better fit.
