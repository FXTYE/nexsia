The quick verdict

Kling AI is the best value pick in AI video for 2026. Kling 3.0's quality is genuinely close to Veo 3 and the discontinued Sora at roughly a third of the per-second cost, and its camera-move controls give you more directorial say than most prompt-only competitors. The tradeoff is audio — if your project needs dialogue or synced sound, you're pairing Kling with a separate tool or picking Veo instead.

Common questions

Kling AI FAQs

Is Kling AI free?
Yes, with 66 credits per day that expire after 24 hours — enough for a couple of short clips before you'd want a paid plan. Paid tiers start at $10/mo (Standard).
Does Kling AI generate audio?
No. Kling clips are silent by default — you'll need to add voice, music or sound effects with a separate tool. This is the main feature gap against Google's Veo 3.
Kling AI vs Veo 3 — which is better?
Kling wins on price and camera-control tools; Veo wins on native audio and physical realism. For high-volume, budget-conscious creation, Kling is the stronger pick. For projects that need dialogue or sound, Veo is worth the premium.
How much does a Kling AI video cost?
Roughly $0.10 per generated second on paid tiers — a 5-second 720p clip runs about 30 credits, which works out cheaper than Veo 3, Runway or the (now discontinued) Sora 2 API.
