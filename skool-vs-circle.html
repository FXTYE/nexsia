Our verdict

For most creators starting or growing their first community, Skool is the better starting point. The price gap is enormous at the entry level — $9/month versus $89/month — and Skool's built-in gamification and one-hour setup let you validate whether a paid community even works before committing real budget to Circle's structure.

Circle earns its place once a community is established and generating consistent revenue, particularly if you need the automation on its Business plan or a fully branded, custom-domain experience. The honest crossover is around $833/month in community revenue — below that, Skool's combination of price and simplicity is hard to beat; above it, do the math on your actual fees before assuming Circle is the "professional" upgrade.

Common questions

Skool vs Circle FAQs

Is Skool or Circle better?
Neither is 'better' outright — Skool wins on price, simplicity and gamified engagement; Circle wins on community structure, branding and automation. Below ~$833/month in community revenue, Skool is the more affordable choice.
Which is cheaper?
Skool's Hobby plan ($9/mo) is nearly 10x cheaper than Circle's entry tier (~$89/mo). But Circle's lower transaction fee (2% vs Skool's 2.9-10%) can save more at higher revenue — the crossover is around $833/month.
Does Circle really cost more than the advertised price?
Often, yes. Circle's pricing page shows annual billing (monthly costs more), doesn't disclose transaction fees upfront, and features like workflow automation are locked behind the $199/month Business plan, not the $89/month entry tier.
Can I run courses on Skool or Circle?
Both host course content, but neither is a full LMS — no quizzes, certificates, drip scheduling or assignments on either platform. If deep course tooling matters, a dedicated platform like Kajabi or Teachable fits better.
Which has a better affiliate program?
Skool includes a 40% recurring affiliate program automatically on every community. Circle doesn't have a native equivalent for community owners to reward referrals.
