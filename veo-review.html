Con

Ultra pricing is a real barrier for daily use

Common questions

Veo 3 FAQs

Is Veo 3 free?
There's a limited free tier through Flow with daily credits, but full access to Veo 3.1 requires a Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) or Ultra ($249.99/mo) subscription.
Does Veo 3 generate sound and dialogue?
Yes — this is Veo 3's headline feature. It generates dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio natively, synced to the video, in the same generation. Most rival models still require a separate audio tool.
Veo 3 vs Kling AI — which is better?
Veo 3 wins on realism and native audio; Kling wins on price and camera-control tools. If sound matters to your project, Veo is the stronger pick. If you're optimizing for cost per generation, Kling is cheaper.
How do I access Veo 3?
Through the Gemini app, Google's Flow filmmaking tool, or Vertex AI for developers — there's no separate standalone "Veo" app. A Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription unlocks full access.
