Our verdict

This comparison splits cleanly along price versus quality. Bluehost's real advantages are the official WordPress.org endorsement and meaningfully lower pricing at every tier, intro and renewal. SiteGround's advantages are broader and more substantial — a dramatically better Trustpilot score (4.7★ vs 3.2★), Google Cloud infrastructure with a measurable performance edge, and WordPress staging included as standard.

If budget is your primary constraint and the WordPress.org endorsement matters to you, Bluehost is a reasonable, defensible choice — just read the checkout screen carefully. But if you can stretch the budget, SiteGround's review-trust gap alone is significant enough that we'd lean toward recommending it for most people building something they intend to keep and grow.

Common questions

Bluehost vs SiteGround FAQs

Is Bluehost or SiteGround better?
SiteGround wins on review trust, support quality, and infrastructure — a real, substantial gap (4.7★ vs 3.2★ on Trustpilot). Bluehost wins on price and carries the official WordPress.org endorsement. If budget is your main constraint, Bluehost is reasonable; otherwise SiteGround is the stronger overall pick.
Which is cheaper?
Bluehost is cheaper at every tier, both intro and renewal — Basic renews at $9.99/mo versus SiteGround StartUp's $14.99-24.99/mo depending on term. The price gap is real and consistent.
Is SiteGround really better for WordPress than Bluehost?
SiteGround includes WordPress staging as standard from GrowBig up, which Bluehost doesn't offer as standard — a genuine developer convenience. That said, Bluehost carries the official WordPress.org endorsement, which SiteGround doesn't have despite its strong WordPress feature set.
Why is Bluehost's Trustpilot score so much lower than SiteGround's?
Reviews consistently point to domain redemption fees and checkout upsells as the main drivers of Bluehost's lower score — a real, documented pattern across a large review sample, not isolated complaints.
Can I switch from Bluehost to SiteGround later?
Yes — SiteGround offers free migration for new customers moving from other hosts. Check Bluehost's domain redemption policy first if you registered your domain through them, since some reviewers report unexpectedly high fees when leaving.
