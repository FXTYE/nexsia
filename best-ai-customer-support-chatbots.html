★★★★½ 4.5 / 5

Common questions

Best AI customer support chatbots — FAQs

What's the best AI customer support platform overall?
Zendesk is our overall pick for 2026 — it offers the deepest customization, largest app marketplace and most scalable reporting of the platforms compared here. If you want similar capability at a lower price, Freshdesk is the stronger value pick.
Which is best for an ecommerce or Shopify store?
Gorgias, by a clear margin — its native Shopify integration surfaces order history, refunds and shipping data directly inside every ticket, and it carries the highest raw G2 rating (4.6/5) of any tool in this comparison.
Which AI customer support tool is cheapest?
ManyChat and Tidio both have free plans and low entry prices ($17/mo and $29/mo respectively), though ManyChat is built for social DM automation rather than traditional ticket-based support. For a real helpdesk, Freshdesk and Zendesk both start at $19/agent/mo.
Which tool has the best AI agent?
Intercom's Fin and Gorgias's AI Agent are both consistently well regarded for resolution quality, at $0.99 and $1.00 per resolution respectively. Zendesk's Advanced AI Agent costs more (~$2/resolution) but is backed by deeper reporting and customization.
What's the difference between ManyChat and the rest of this list?
ManyChat is a social and messaging marketing automation platform, not a traditional helpdesk — its strength is Instagram comment-to-DM automation and multi-channel campaign flows, not ticket-based customer support. It's included here because it gets searched alongside these tools constantly, but it solves a genuinely different problem.
Which is best for a small business on a tight budget?
Tidio — it's the fastest and cheapest way to get a live chat widget running on a small site, though its Lyro AI chatbot is a separate add-on rather than bundled into the base plan.

We research real 2026 pricing and AI billing models, test feature depth hands-on, and factor in how transparent each platform's true monthly cost is — since AI agent pricing varies wildly across this category. We disclose affiliate links and stay upfront about real limitations.
