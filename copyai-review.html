The quick verdict

Copy.ai is no longer the cheap copywriting tool its reputation is built on. It repositioned into an enterprise "go-to-market" platform — chat, templates, brand voice and workflow automation that chains multi-step content and even sales-prospecting tasks together. That's genuinely powerful for a marketing team with a real budget, and genuinely the wrong tool for a solo creator who just wants help writing captions.

Be honest with your readers here: the $29/mo Chat plan gets you unlimited chat and templates, but the actual "GTM platform" — Workflows, Agents, CRM integrations — sits behind Growth-tier pricing starting around $1,000/month. Match the recommendation to what someone's actually trying to do.

Know before you buy

The GTM pivot, honestly

This is the detail most "Copy.ai review" articles gloss over, because they were written before the repositioning. Copy.ai began in 2020 as a simple short-form copy generator. By 2024 it had rebranded around "Go-To-Market AI" — adding sales prospecting, lead research, CRM enrichment and deal coaching on top of its original writing tools.

The real automation lives behind $1,000+/month pricing

The entry Chat plan ($29/mo) gives you unlimited chat, 90+ templates and basic workflow templates — genuinely useful for a solo writer. But the multi-step Workflows, Agents and CRM integrations that define the "GTM platform" positioning sit on the Growth plan and above, starting around $1,000/month billed annually. If you're comparing Copy.ai to Jasper or ChatGPT as a writing tool, you're likely looking at the wrong tier.

The pivot also shows up in user sentiment: Copy.ai holds a strong 4.4★ on G2, largely from reviews written in its earlier, cheaper era — but sits at just 1.9★ on Trustpilot, where complaints about the pricing changes and the shift away from simple copywriting are common. Both numbers are real; they're just measuring different eras of the same product.

Common questions

Copy.ai FAQs

Is Copy.ai still just a copywriting tool?
No — it repositioned in 2024 into a "Go-To-Market AI" platform, adding sales prospecting, CRM enrichment and workflow automation on top of its original writing tools. The $29/mo Chat plan still works well for basic copywriting, though.
Is Copy.ai free?
There's a free plan with 2,000 words per month and one user seat — enough to test the interface, not enough for regular content production.
Why is Copy.ai so expensive now?
The entry Chat plan is still $29/month, but the actual workflow automation and GTM features that justify the "platform" positioning start around $1,000/month on the Growth tier — a big jump most reviews don't make clear upfront.
Copy.ai vs Jasper — which is better for writing?
Jasper is stronger for pure long-form writing with its document editor. Copy.ai's advantage is workflow automation — if you want to chain multi-step content processes, Copy.ai wins; for a polished writing environment, Jasper is worth a look.
Why the gap between G2 and Trustpilot ratings?
Copy.ai's 4.4★ G2 score is largely from its earlier, cheaper era as a copywriting tool. Its 1.9★ Trustpilot score reflects more recent complaints about the pricing changes and the shift toward enterprise GTM features.
