The quick verdict

ProtonVPN earns its spot for one reason above all others: it's the only VPN in this comparison with a genuinely free, unlimited-time plan backed by an independently audited no-logs policy. That combination is rare — most "free" VPNs cut corners on privacy to make money elsewhere. The free tier is deliberately limited (one device, fewer server locations, slower speeds) to encourage upgrading, but as a zero-cost way to actually trust a VPN, nothing else here comes close. Paid plans are competitively priced too, just with a smaller server network than NordVPN or Surfshark.

Common questions

ProtonVPN FAQs

Is ProtonVPN actually free?
Yes — the free plan has no data cap and no expiry date, unlike most "free" VPN trials. It's limited to one device and a smaller set of server locations, but it's a genuinely usable, ongoing free tier.
Is ProtonVPN's no-logs policy actually audited?
Yes — Proton has commissioned independent third-party audits of its no-logs claims more than once, which is a higher bar than most VPN providers meet.
ProtonVPN vs NordVPN — which is better?
ProtonVPN wins if you want a genuinely free tier and audited privacy; NordVPN wins on raw speed and server network size. If budget and trust matter most, go ProtonVPN. If speed and scale matter most, go NordVPN.
Does ProtonVPN work with Netflix?
On paid plans, yes for many regions — though NordVPN and Surfshark are generally more consistently reliable for unblocking streaming platforms.
How many devices can I use with ProtonVPN?
One device on the free plan, up to 10 on the Plus and Unlimited paid tiers.
