The quick verdict

NordVPN is our overall pick for 2026 because it's the rare VPN that's both the fastest in independent testing and backed by the largest, most reliable server network of the tools we compared. Under the hood

Key features

Threat Protection

Blocks ads, trackers and known-malicious sites at the network level, even when the VPN itself is off.

Meshnet

Securely connect your own devices directly to each other over an encrypted peer-to-peer tunnel.

Double VPN & Onion over VPN

Extra-hardened routing options for the most privacy-sensitive use cases.

Kill switch & split tunneling

Blocks internet traffic if the VPN drops, and lets you choose which apps use the tunnel.

NordPass & Data Breach Scanner

Password manager and breach monitoring bundled into the Complete and Prime tiers.

10 simultaneous connections

Common questions

NordVPN FAQs

Is NordVPN free?
No — there's no free tier, only a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. If you want a genuinely free VPN to try first, ProtonVPN's free plan is the better starting point.
Is NordVPN actually the fastest VPN?
In independent 2026 speed testing, yes — NordVPN hit 857 Mbps, ahead of every other VPN in this comparison, including ExpressVPN's 808 Mbps.
NordVPN vs Surfshark — which is better?
NordVPN wins on raw speed and server network size; Surfshark wins on price and unlimited simultaneous devices. If you have a lot of devices to cover or want the cheapest rate, Surfshark is the stronger pick. If speed and server choice matter most, go with NordVPN.
Does NordVPN work with Netflix?
Yes — NordVPN is one of the more reliable VPNs for unblocking US Netflix and other major streaming platforms, alongside Surfshark.
How many devices can I use with one NordVPN account?
Up to 10 simultaneous connections on any plan. If you need more, Surfshark's unlimited-device policy is the better fit.
