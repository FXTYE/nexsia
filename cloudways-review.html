The quick verdict

Cloudways solves the exact problem this entire category struggles with — no renewal shock, because there's no promotional intro rate to expire in the first place. You pay for the server resources you actually use, across your choice of five cloud providers, and a strong 4.7★ G2 rating backs up genuinely good performance and infrastructure quality.

The honest caveat: this isn't a beginner-friendly, all-in-one host. There's no cPanel, no file manager, no included email hosting or free domain — you're managing a cloud server through Cloudways' dashboard, not a traditional shared-hosting control panel. Support quality is also genuinely inconsistent across reviews — some describe sub-90-second first responses, others describe 24+ hour delays with repeated escalation. If you're comfortable with that trade-off and want pricing that won't surprise you at renewal, Cloudways is a strong, defensible pick — just go in knowing it's built for a more technical audience than Hostinger, SiteGround or Bluehost.

Know before you buy

The no-cPanel gap, honestly

This is the detail that catches people coming from traditional shared hosting off guard. Cloudways deliberately doesn't include cPanel, a file manager, email hosting, or a free domain — it's a cloud server management layer, not a full-service hosting panel, and that's a genuine trade-off, not an oversight.

No email hosting is included at any tier

If you need business email (you@yourdomain.com), you'll need a separate service like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 — Cloudways genuinely doesn't offer it, unlike Hostinger, SiteGround or Bluehost, which all bundle at least basic email. Budget for this as a separate line item if it matters to your setup.

Two more things worth knowing: support response times are genuinely inconsistent across reviews — some describe sub-90-second first responses on live chat, others describe 24+ hour delays with repeated escalation before reaching someone who can actually solve the problem. And there's no phone support on standard plans — Pro-tier plans get faster response times and access to senior engineers, but that's an upgrade, not the default.

Common questions

Cloudways FAQs

Is Cloudways good for beginners?
Not really — there's no cPanel, no file manager, and no email hosting included. If you're a first-time site owner, Hostinger, SiteGround or Bluehost will feel more familiar. Cloudways is built for developers and agencies who want cloud infrastructure without managing servers directly.
Does Cloudways have a renewal price jump?
No — this is Cloudways' key differentiator in this category. Pricing is pay-as-you-go based on actual resource usage, so there's no promotional intro rate to expire and no renewal shock like Hostinger, SiteGround or Bluehost.
Which cloud provider should I choose on Cloudways?
DigitalOcean Premium ($14/mo) is the recommended starting point for most standard blogs or business sites — faster processors than the base Standard tier. AWS and Google Cloud cost roughly 3x more but offer enterprise-grade infrastructure for high-traffic or resource-intensive applications.
Does Cloudways include email hosting?
No — Cloudways doesn't offer email hosting or a free domain at any tier. You'll need a separate service like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for business email.
Is Cloudways support reliable?
Reviews are genuinely mixed. Many describe fast, knowledgeable support with response times under 90 seconds; others describe slow first responses and repeated escalation. Pro-tier plans get faster response times and access to senior engineers.
