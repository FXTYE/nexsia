About Nexsia

We built the site we wished existed before we wasted $400 on the wrong tool.

Nexsia is an independent research site that compares the software running online businesses — head to head, with real numbers, and an honest verdict even when it's inconvenient for us.

Most "best tool" articles online are written to sell you something. They rank whoever pays the highest commission, they copy the same five sentences from each other's affiliate pages, and they almost never tell you which option is genuinely the worse deal. We got tired of reading them — so we started writing something else instead.

Our mission

Give anyone comparing business software the same clarity a smart friend who'd already tried everything would give you over coffee — the real pricing, the honest trade-offs, and a straight answer to "just tell me which one."

Our vision

A web where comparing software doesn't mean wading through ten near-identical "Top 10" posts to find one honest sentence. We want Nexsia to be the place people bookmark before they buy — not the fifth tab they close in frustration.

"If every tool on this site scored a 4.8, the scores would be worthless. So we don't let that happen."

What we actually believe

The right answer is often "it depends"

We'd rather tell you which tool fits your specific situation than pretend one option wins for everyone.

Commissions can't buy a ranking

We earn affiliate revenue from some tools we recommend. It never decides which one we recommend.

Specific beats generic

"Great for teams" tells you nothing. A real crossover price, a real fee structure — that's what changes a decision.

Stale numbers are worse than none

Software pricing changes constantly. We date every review and revisit them, rather than letting them quietly go wrong.

How Nexsia actually gets made

1We research like we're buying it ourselves

Real pricing pages, real feature lists, real complaints on Trustpilot and Reddit — not a rewritten version of someone else's article.

2We look for the number that actually matters

Not "it's affordable" — the specific revenue point where one tool's fees start costing more than the other's subscription. That's the sentence that earns your trust.

3We write the verdict we'd want to read

Including who a tool is wrong for. Especially that part.
