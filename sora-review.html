The quick verdict

Sora's output quality was never really the problem — its availability is. OpenAI discontinued the standalone Sora web and iOS app on April 26, 2026, folded remaining access into ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscriptions, and has now confirmed the Sora 2 API stops taking requests on September 24, 2026. If you're starting a new project today, we'd point you at Veo 3 or Kling AI instead — both are actively developed, more predictably accessible, and closer in quality than the search buzz around Sora would suggest.

If you already have ChatGPT Plus or Pro and just want to try it before the door closes further, it's still worth a look — just don't build a workflow around it.

Common questions

Sora FAQs

Is Sora shutting down?
The standalone Sora consumer app and website were discontinued on April 26, 2026. The Sora 2 API is also being sunset — OpenAI has confirmed it stops accepting requests on September 24, 2026. Video generation continues to exist inside ChatGPT Plus/Pro, but the dedicated Sora product is gone.
How do I access Sora now?
Through a ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Pro ($200/mo) subscription. There's no free tier and no separate Sora app or site to sign up for anymore.
Is Sora still the best AI video model?
On raw photorealism, it's still competitive with Veo and Kling. But "best" has to account for access — an actively developed model you can actually build on, like Veo 3 or Kling AI, is the more practical choice for anything beyond a one-off experiment.
Sora vs Veo 3 — which should I use?
For a new project today, Veo 3. It matches Sora on realism, adds native audio, and is on an active development roadmap rather than a wind-down timeline. See Sora vs Veo 3 for the full comparison.
