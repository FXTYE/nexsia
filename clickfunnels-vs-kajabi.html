In depth

Pros & cons of each platform

CF ClickFunnels

Pro

The best funnel builder, full stop

More page types, deeper A/B testing and more conditional branching than Kajabi, with multi-step checkouts and dynamic upsell/downsell paths handled elegantly. If conversion is the goal, this is the specialist tool.

Pro

Affiliate centre on every plan

Built-in affiliate management — including a two-tier system where affiliates earn from referring other affiliates — is available even on the entry plan, not locked behind a higher tier.

Pro

Lower entry price

Starts around $97/mo, below Kajabi's entry plan, with a 14-day free trial to test first.

Con

Course & membership tools are basic

Course hosting was bolted on with ClickFunnels 2.0 and remains significantly less capable than Kajabi's. Course limits are tight — even higher tiers cap course counts where Kajabi allows far more.

Con

The 2.0 migration was rocky

The move from ClickFunnels Classic to 2.0 drew widespread reports of bugs and missing features. It has stabilised since, but it's a fair thing to be aware of going in.

K Kajabi

Pro

Best-in-class course delivery

Quizzes, certificates, drip content, memberships and a genuine student mobile app. For a knowledge business, the student experience is well ahead of ClickFunnels.

Pro

Truly all-in-one

Email marketing, website, course hosting and community are included on every plan — you're not stitching separate tools together.

Pro

Far better course economics at scale

Its higher tiers allow many more courses for the money, so multi-course creators pay dramatically less per course than on ClickFunnels' comparable plans.

Con

Higher starting price

The entry plan is roughly $149–179/mo — more than ClickFunnels — and limits you to a handful of products and funnels before you need to upgrade.

Con

Affiliate program is gated

Built-in affiliate marketing only unlocks on the Growth plan (~$199/mo) and above — and the entry plan keeps Kajabi branding.

One detail most comparisons skip: both platforms add a surcharge when you use your own Stripe account — Kajabi around 2% on Basic and 1% on Growth, ClickFunnels about 1% across plans — on top of Stripe's standard fees. Factor that in at higher revenue.

Feature by feature

What each platform gives you

Funnel & page builder

ClickFunnels: deep, flexible, conversion-focused. Kajabi: capable pipeline builder, but less optimised for complex funnels.

Courses & memberships

Kajabi: full LMS — drip, quizzes, certificates, community. ClickFunnels: basic course hosting with tight limits.

Email marketing

Kajabi: built-in on every plan with solid automation. ClickFunnels: email included, more funnel-centric.

Community & mobile app

Kajabi: native community plus a branded student mobile app. ClickFunnels: no equivalent mobile app.

Upsells & order bumps

ClickFunnels: one-click upsells, downsells and order bumps are a core strength. Kajabi: present but less sophisticated.

Affiliate management

ClickFunnels: on all plans, two-tier. Kajabi: Growth plan and up only.

Integrations

Both connect to Zapier and webhooks for almost anything; ClickFunnels edges ahead on direct integrations.

Free trial

Both offer a 14-day free trial. Neither has a permanent free plan (if you need free, look at Systeme.io).

Our verdict

They're not really competitors — they fit different business models. ClickFunnels is the pick if your revenue comes from funnels: driving traffic to an offer through opt-in, sales and upsell pages. Kajabi is the pick if your revenue is courses, memberships and community, where its delivery and student experience are in a league of their own.

The honest test: what does your business sell? Offers through funnels → ClickFunnels. Courses and coaching → Kajabi. If you genuinely need both deep funnels and lead management, it's worth also looking at an all-in-one like GoHighLevel.

Common questions

ClickFunnels vs Kajabi FAQs

Is ClickFunnels or Kajabi better?
Neither is 'better' outright — they do different jobs. ClickFunnels is better for sales funnels; Kajabi is better for courses and community. Match it to what your business actually sells.
Which is cheaper?
ClickFunnels' entry funnel plan (~$97/mo) starts lower than Kajabi's Basic (~$179/mo), but they're not like-for-like — you're paying for a funnel builder vs a full course platform.
Can Kajabi build funnels?
It has basic marketing and page tools, but it's not a dedicated funnel builder. If funnels are central to your business, ClickFunnels (or an all-in-one) does it better.
Can ClickFunnels host courses?
It added course features, but they're basic next to Kajabi's. A serious course business should lead with a course platform, not a funnel builder.
