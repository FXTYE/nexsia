The quick verdict

Surfshark is our value pick for 2026 because it removes the one restriction that annoys people most about VPNs — device limits — while still undercutting NordVPN and ExpressVPN on price. Speeds and server count aren't quite at NordVPN's level, but the gap rarely matters for everyday streaming, browsing or torrenting. If you're covering a household full of phones, laptops and streaming boxes on one account, Surfshark is the easiest recommendation in this whole category.

Common questions

Surfshark FAQs

Is Surfshark free?
No — there's no free tier, only a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. ProtonVPN is the better option if you want a genuinely free VPN to try first.
Does Surfshark really allow unlimited devices?
Yes — every Surfshark plan allows unlimited simultaneous device connections, unlike NordVPN (10) or ExpressVPN's tiered 10–14 limit.
Surfshark vs NordVPN — which is better?
Surfshark wins on price and unlimited devices; NordVPN wins on raw speed and server network size. If you have a lot of devices to cover or want the cheapest rate, go Surfshark. If speed and server choice matter most, go NordVPN.
Does Surfshark work with Netflix?
Yes — Surfshark is one of the more reliable VPNs for unblocking US Netflix and other major streaming platforms.
How many devices can I use with one Surfshark account?
Unlimited — there's no device cap on any plan, which is unusual among major VPN providers.
