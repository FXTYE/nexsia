Common questions

PDF to JPG FAQs

Is this really free?
Yes — there's no signup, no watermark, no page limit and no hidden paywall. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there's no server cost on our end to pass on to you.
Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?
No. The conversion happens entirely on your device using JavaScript in your browser. Your file is never sent to Nexsia or any third party — closing the tab is the same as deleting it.
Is there a file size or page limit?
No hard limit, though very large PDFs (100+ pages, or very high-resolution scans) will take longer and use more memory since your device is doing the work instead of a server. For most documents, conversion is fast.
Does this work on my phone?
Yes — you can select a PDF from your phone's files app and convert it, all from the mobile browser. Downloading many pages as a ZIP works on most mobile browsers too.
Can I convert a password-protected PDF?
Not currently — encrypted or password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked first before their pages can be rendered. Unprotected PDFs of any other kind work fine.
What quality setting should I use?
High works well for most documents — a good balance of clarity and file size. Use Maximum for documents with fine text or detailed graphics you need to zoom into, and Standard when you just need a quick, smaller image.
