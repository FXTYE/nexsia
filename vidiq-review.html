The quick verdict

vidIQ is the strongest keyword-research tool for YouTubers in 2026, and worth the Boost plan if your growth depends on ranking in search — tutorials, how-tos, reviews and explainers. It's weaker at turning that research into packaging, its AI Coach leans generic, and the credit system runs down fast. If you mainly need A/B testing or bulk tools, TubeBuddy is the better buy.

Common questions

vidIQ FAQs

Is vidIQ free?
Yes, vidIQ has a permanent free plan with 150 AI credits per month, the core Chrome extension stats, and limited keyword and Daily Ideas access. It doubles as the trial — most creators upgrade to the ~$16.58/month Boost plan for full keyword data.
Is vidIQ worth it in 2026?
For search-driven channels, yes — the keyword research is the best in the category and worth the Boost plan. If you mainly need A/B testing or bulk tools, TubeBuddy gives you more for less.
vidIQ vs TubeBuddy — which is better?
vidIQ wins on keyword research and AI ideation; TubeBuddy wins on price, A/B testing and bulk tools. Many creators run vidIQ free for research alongside a paid TubeBuddy plan.
Is vidIQ safe for my channel?
Yes. vidIQ is an official YouTube partner tool that uses the YouTube API, trusted by millions of creators. It doesn't violate YouTube's terms.
What are AI credits?
vidIQ meters its AI features (Daily Ideas, AI Coach, generation) in monthly credits. Free gives 150, Boost 2,000 and Max 6,000. Heavy or daily users can exhaust lower allowances quickly.
