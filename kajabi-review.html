The quick verdict

Kajabi is still the most polished, easiest-to-use platform for selling courses, coaching and memberships — content delivery, student experience, and the guided setup process are genuinely ahead of most competitors, and it replaces a real stack of separate tools.

But the value calculation has shifted. A January 2026 restructure pushed Basic from $149 to $179/mo while cutting its contact limit from 10,000 to 2,500, and Trustpilot reviews describe previously-free features (like subtitles) being pulled and resold as paid add-ons. If your business is funnels first, ClickFunnels is the better fit — Kajabi is the pick specifically when courses and community are the product, not just an add-on.

Know before you buy

The January 2026 price restructure, honestly

This is the detail most reviews still get wrong, because it happened mid-year and many older articles haven't caught up. In January 2026, Kajabi raised every plan and quietly removed the old entry-level "Kickstarter" tier from its public pricing page.

Basic jumped 20% and lost two-thirds of its contact allowance

Basic rose from $149 to $179/mo, Growth from $199 to $249/mo, and Pro from $399 to $499/mo — while Basic's contact limit was cut from 10,000 down to just 2,500. The $89/mo Kickstarter plan still technically exists (reachable through specific signup links) but is capped at 1 product and 250 contacts, and most creators outgrow it within weeks.

Two more costs worth budgeting for: Kajabi Payments charges 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction, and if you use your own Stripe account instead, Kajabi adds its own surcharge on top — 2% on Basic, 1% on Growth, 0.5% on Pro (PayPal is exempt). And because Kajabi charges per website, running more than one course brand means paying for a full separate subscription for each — this adds up fast for multi-brand creators.

Common questions

Kajabi FAQs

Is Kajabi worth $179+/month in 2026?
Yes, if courses, coaching or memberships are your core business and you want the best student experience without hiring a developer. It's a harder sell for hobbyists, tight budgets, or anyone running multiple course brands, since Kajabi bills per website.
Why did Kajabi get more expensive?
Kajabi restructured pricing in January 2026 — Basic rose from $149 to $179/mo (while its contact limit was cut from 10,000 to 2,500), Growth from $199 to $249/mo, and Pro from $399 to $499/mo. The old $89/mo Kickstarter plan was removed from public pricing.
Does Kajabi charge transaction fees?
Kajabi Payments charges 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction. If you connect your own Stripe account instead, Kajabi adds its own surcharge on top — 2% on Basic, 1% on Growth, 0.5% on Pro. PayPal transactions are exempt from the surcharge.
Kajabi vs ClickFunnels — which is better?
They're built for different jobs — Kajabi for courses and community, ClickFunnels for sales funnels. If your revenue comes from course sales and student delivery, Kajabi wins; if it comes from funnels and upsells, ClickFunnels wins. See our full comparison.
Can I run more than one course brand on Kajabi?
Yes, but each website is billed separately — there's no multi-brand discount. Two course brands means two full subscriptions, which changes the economics considerably for creators scaling into new niches.
Is there a free version of Kajabi?
No permanent free plan, but there's a 14-day free trial. Thinkific offers a genuine free tier if you want to test the course-platform model before paying anything.
