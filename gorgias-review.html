The quick verdict

Gorgias earns the highest raw satisfaction score in this comparison, and it's easy to see why for ecommerce: nothing else here surfaces Shopify order history, refunds and shipping edits as seamlessly inside a support ticket. Unlimited agent seats is a genuine advantage for stores with seasonal staffing swings. The trade-off is Gorgias's core pricing model — per ticket rather than per seat — which means a viral sale or a bad shipping week can spike your bill in a way seat-based competitors don't.

Common questions

Gorgias FAQs

Is Gorgias free?
No — there's no permanent free plan, only a free trial. Starter begins at $10/mo for 50 tickets.
How does Gorgias's per-ticket pricing work?
Each plan includes a monthly ticket allowance (50 on Starter up to 5,000+ on Advanced); overage tickets are billed individually, and agent seats are unlimited on every tier.
How much does the Gorgias AI Agent cost?
$1.00 per resolution on monthly billing, or $0.90 on annual billing, with overages above your plan's included volume billed at $1.50 each.
Gorgias vs Zendesk — which is better for ecommerce?
Gorgias, clearly — its native Shopify integration puts order and shipping data directly inside tickets, which Zendesk can only approach through third-party app marketplace integrations.
Is Gorgias only for Shopify stores?
It's built with Shopify as the primary integration, but also supports BigCommerce, Magento and other ecommerce platforms, just with somewhat less native depth than the Shopify integration.
